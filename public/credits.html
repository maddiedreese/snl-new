A real cork photograph

The board uses Empty corkboard at the end of the semester by Josephine Barham, used under the Unsplash License. Its tone is adjusted to complement the cards. The photograph appears once, without tiling.

Custom lettering and artwork

Studio Marker is a custom, locally hosted font built by studying individual letterforms in announcement-card references. No proprietary font file is used. The paper and push pins are drawn by the site. The typography and illustrations are inspired by the announcement-board format; they are not official NBC assets.

Your cards stay yours

Your text and finished image are composed in your browser. This site has no image-generation API, user accounts, or upload service. No generated cork textures are used.
